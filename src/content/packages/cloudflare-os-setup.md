## Why this works for a small business

### Your team gets AI tooling, not just an AI tool

Most SMBs give employees a single chatbot subscription and hope for the best. Cloudflare OS
is a platform: your team can build small automations and integrations themselves, on
infrastructure that's actually governed, instead of pasting sensitive data into a browser tab.

### Guardrails built in from day one

Small businesses rarely have a security team to review every new AI tool. I configure spend
limits, access scoping, and audit trails as part of setup, so you get the upside of self-serve
AI without an open-ended bill or an unmonitored data leak.

### You own it — no lock-in

Everything I build lives in your Cloudflare tenant and your own source repositories. If you
never hire me again, the platform and every automation on it keeps working and keeps belonging
to you.
