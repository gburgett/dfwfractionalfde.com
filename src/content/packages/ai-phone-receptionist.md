## Why this works for a small business

### Staffing a phone line is expensive for a small team

A receptionist costs real salary and benefits, and still can't cover nights, weekends, or
lunch breaks. An AI receptionist answers every call immediately, at a fraction of the cost,
so a missed call never becomes a lost customer.

### It's a real conversation, not an IVR maze

"Press 1 for sales" trains customers to hang up. Callers talk to the agent in plain language,
get routed correctly, and can leave detailed messages — a noticeably better experience for
the person calling your business.

### It knows when to hand off to a person

Every workflow is built with clear escalation paths: urgent issues get routed to on-call
staff immediately, and the agent never pretends to be human or promises something it can't
do. You get automation where it helps and a human where it matters.
