## Angle

Flagship post. The higher price is buying three things the cheaper system makes you build yourself: opinionated workflows, integrations that already exist, and industry knowledge encoded in the product. The interface barely matters when AI is doing the work on top of the system. The comparison that matters is the cost of rebuilding that knowledge yourself versus the difference in the license.

The vendor this is leaning toward is Zywave. The relevant public facts to use, and no others: Zywave started as an agency, went all in on AI, and offers an open MCP server. That is the concrete version of "built for software to work on top of it," not a nicer set of screens.
