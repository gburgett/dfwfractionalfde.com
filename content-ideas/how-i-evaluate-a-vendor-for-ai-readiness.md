## Angle

Evergreen blog post and lead magnet. A rubric an owner can take into any software evaluation, written for SMB owners whose MSP will not do this work.

Combine the other AMS notes into one checklist, with no vendor and no client attached:

1. **Openness.** Is there an MCP server, or a modern API with good docs? Having an API is not the same as being open. Can you get data out and act on it, not only read it? See `built-for-the-point-and-click-era.md`.
2. **Opinionated data model.** A blank, endlessly customizable system hands you the job of encoding how your industry works. A database is not a product. See `a-database-isnt-a-product.md`.
3. **Workflows out of the box.** Does it already know how the work runs, or will you pay to rebuild that?
4. **Integrations.** Start from the ones you cannot live without, then look at the feature list. See `integration-is-the-real-requirement.md`. Keep this section general. Do not use the named benefits platform in this public post.
5. **The vendor's AI direction.** Are they building so AI can work on top of the system, or so people can click nicer screens? The UI is not the point. See `the-ui-is-not-the-point-anymore.md`.
6. **Total cost, including the rebuild.** Compare the license with the cost of recreating workflows, integrations, and industry knowledge yourself. A higher price can be the cheaper path. No numbers, and no "which vendor we picked."

The close is the offer: if your MSP will not run this evaluation, a fractional engineer can sit in the demos with you and write down the answers.
