## Angle

A system that is endlessly customizable, and not opinionated, hands you the job of encoding how your industry works. That is a database with a login, not a product.

You want software that already knows how an agency runs, or that ships workflows you can use on day one. Otherwise you are paying twice: once for the license, and again for the years of industry knowledge someone still has to type in.
