## Angle

For SMB owners whose MSP never does this. Running an AI agent over a system is useful when you treat the result as a map of visibility: what the agent could inspect, and what it could not see at all.

The list of blind spots is the deliverable. It tells the owner which parts of the business have no one looking, which is often the honest state when IT is an MSP that isn't creative or responsive.
