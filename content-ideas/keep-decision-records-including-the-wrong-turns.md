## Angle

Thought-partner piece for owners. Keep a record of decisions, and keep the wrong turns in the record too. The next decision needs to see why the last one changed.

Public example: ADR 0039 in kroger-mealplanner supersedes six earlier decision records. The superseded ones stay. They are how you tell a later reader that the current choice was earned, not obvious.

For an owner this is the same habit as a good advisor: write down what you decided, what you tried first, and why you stopped. The AI work goes better when that history is in the room.
