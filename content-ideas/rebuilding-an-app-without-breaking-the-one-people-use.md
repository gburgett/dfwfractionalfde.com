## Angle

A change-management lesson for owners. You can rebuild an app without taking the working one away from the people who depend on it.

- New screens sit behind a per-organization flag.
- An unfinished screen, or a screen that fails to load, falls back to the old version.
- Switching a customer over is a data change, not a release.

The owner stays in control of who sees the new version, and a bad day for the new screens does not stop the business.
