## Angle

The meal planner keeps the assistant's working material as documents in a database. That is a plain analogy for a company knowledgebase: the AI answers from the company's own documents, not from a general model's memory.

This is the knowledgebase offer in miniature. Procedures, prices, and history live in a place the owner can read and correct. The assistant reads those documents. When something is wrong, you fix the document.

Fills a gap in current content. Knowledgebases are a core part of the work, and the public notes don't yet walk through what one is.
