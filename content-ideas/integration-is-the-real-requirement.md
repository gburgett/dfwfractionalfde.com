## Angle

The business driver for this evaluation was a specific integration: Employee Navigator, a benefits administration platform. The feature grid was secondary. If the system of record cannot meet the integration the business already depends on, the rest of the demo does not matter.

The owner-facing lesson: start a software evaluation from the integrations you cannot live without, then look at the feature list. An AMS, a CRM, or any other system of record is only as useful as the work it can do with the tools you already run.
